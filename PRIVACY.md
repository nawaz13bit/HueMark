# HueMark Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

HueMark does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

## What HueMark stores, and where

- Your search terms, their assigned colors, and your settings (whole-word
  matching, colorblind palette, toolbar position, etc.) are saved using the
  browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API.
- This data is stored **only on your own device**. It is never synced to a
  server, sent to HueMark's developer, or shared with any third party.
- Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

## What HueMark does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No external servers of any kind — HueMark makes no network requests on
  your behalf.
- No advertising or ad networks.
- No sale or sharing of data, because none is collected in the first place.

## Permissions

HueMark requests broad permissions (`storage`, `scripting`, `activeTab`,
`declarativeNetRequest`, and host access to all sites) purely to let it
highlight text on any page or PDF you choose to use it on, and to redirect
PDF navigations to its bundled PDF.js viewer (needed because browsers don't
allow third-party scripts inside their built-in PDF viewer). None of these
permissions are used to collect or transmit data.
