Advanced: export & citations

A paragraph or sentence that contains several of your terms is listed once, under a header such as "cat + dog", instead of being repeated under each term. Nothing is duplicated, the totals stay honest, and the "cat + dog" section shows where your terms appear together. Untick this to repeat the passage under every term it contains.

PDF help

"Open PDF viewer" opens a tab where you can open a PDF file from your computer with the viewer's Open File button. PDFs opened from web links load in this viewer automatically; for PDFs already on your computer, enable "Allow access to file URLs" for HueMark in the browser's extension settings (Chrome/Edge).

Export help

Format: Matches lists every hit. Citation adds a formatted reference (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA, Bluebook, OSCOLA, McGill, AGLC, SCC/AIR, BGH/BVerfG or Plain) and an in-text citation after each quote. Research adds the author, date, source and section for each passage. Resume shows which terms were found, with counts, and where. Click the button once to collect the content (it may take a moment on long pages or PDFs); when it reads “Export ready: click to copy”, click it again to copy the result, then paste it where you need it.

Saved lists help

Save your current terms under a name to reuse them later. You can keep up to 10 lists; click Delete twice to remove one and make room for another.
